Terms of use.

An information website for personal projects, with explicit limits on what its descriptions establish.

Revision: 13 October 2026

Project and website

Quiet Field Notes provides information about personal projects and research themes. Learning Engine is an experimental personal project; its legacy Google Drive/n8n bridge has been retired. This website does not offer public enrollment, private learning-data access, or a paid service.

Information and interpretation

Retrieved or generated information may be incomplete or incorrect. Check important claims against original sources. The presence of a paper, note, or retrieved record does not establish mastery. A project outline does not establish implementation, validation, or suitability for a particular use.

Operation and availability

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Availability depends on its hosting provider and the visitor’s network. No service-level, continuous-availability, or regional-access guarantee is offered. The separate Sites display pilot does not establish long-term availability or direct access from every network.

The website does not operate the owner’s private learning services. A website release does not establish their current deployment or acceptance.

Materials and privacy

The owner controls materials submitted to private learning tools and must have the necessary rights to process them. This website is informational; do not post private material in the public issue tracker.

See the privacy policy for website hosting and historical integration data, retention, and revoking access.
